Title: US Traffic Ticket Courts and Payment Portals

Description / Abstract:
A machine-readable reference for what to do after a US traffic ticket: 153 city records across
21 states, plus 21 state records. Each city record names the court or agency that actually handles
the citation and links to that agency's own official payment or contest portal - no third-party
payment services - plus the fine structure, the point consequences, contest options, and the facts
that are still unverified. Each state record covers the point system and whether a traffic course
dismisses the case, withholds adjudication, only reduces points, or does not exist.
